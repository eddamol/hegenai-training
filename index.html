About This Page

HEGenAI Training is a platform for short, practical training modules for educators in higher education, on using generative AI in assessment and feedback. It is for educators in higher education, from any institution or discipline. It is developed as a part of a research project, and two events are upcoming: A pilot and a training session.

Why Participate?

As a higher education educator, you can make a small time investment that leads to something useful on both sides. You spend about an hour learning one practical way to use GenAI in a real assessment and feedback situation - something you should be able to use in practice after the hour. That is your benefit. And for the educators that follow, your participation and input can help shape the training modules before they roll out at a larger scale.

Who can participate?

Any higher education educator who is actively working with feedback and assessment or will do so within a year, from any discipline or country. No prior AI experience needed.

Where will the sessions be?

Both sessions will most likely be hybrid, with some participants at DTU, Lyngby, and some online.

Should I do the pilot or the training?

Pilot if you want to be among the first to react to a pilot training module (no follow-up work). Training if you want to be in the second round, apply what you learned, and hand in a simple work template.

What exactly will I learn?

The topics have not been confirmed, but possible topics include ‘disclosure of GenAI use in written assignments’ and ‘GenAI for making effective multiple choice assessments.’ Got topic suggestions? Feel free to contact the researcher via email: s250265@dtu.dk

The Pilot & Training

There are two ways to take part. Sign-up will open soon.

Pilot

  • Try out a draft training module
  • Give feedback in a short, anonymous survey
  • No work to submit and no certification

Training

  • Take the training session
  • Use what you learned in your own teaching
  • Submit a simple activity template
  • Fill in a short survey before and after

Who Is Behind This?

HEGenAI Training is run by Edda Möller, an MSc student at DTU, supervised by Md Saifuddin Khalid. It is a part of her thesis project, carried out alongside the Erasmus+ funded project HEGenAI. The full thesis title is Design, Implementation, and Impact Evaluation of Generative AI Training for Assessment and Feedback in Higher Education: A Design-Based Research Study. Both the pilot and the training feed back into the design of the training.

Questions? Write to Edda: s250265@dtu.dk
