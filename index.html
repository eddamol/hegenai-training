About This Page

HEGenAI Training is a platform for short, practical training modules for educators in higher education, on using generative AI in assessment and feedback. It is for educators in higher education, from any institution or discipline. The platform is currently being built and will host two events in November 2026: A pilot and training.

The Pilot & Training

Any higher education educator can participate in either the pilot or training. Sign-up will open soon. Note: Dates and details may change.

Pilot

Planned: Mon 2 Nov 2026

  • Try out a draft training module
  • Give feedback in a short, anonymous survey
  • No evidence to submit and no certification

Training

Planned: Mon 16 Nov + Thu 26 Nov 2026

  • A short survey before and after
  • Take the training
  • Apply a learning and submit simple evidence
  • Join the 30-minute debrief

Dates

Note: Dates and details may change.

  1. Pilot

    Mon 2 Nov 2026

    Try out a draft training module and give feedback. About an hour long.

  2. Training session

    Mon 16 Nov 2026

    Take the training. About an hour long.

    16-26 Nov 2026

  4. Debrief

Who Is Behind This?

HEGenAI Training is run by Edda Möller, an MSc student at DTU, supervised by Md Saifuddin Khalid. It is a part of her thesis project, carried out alongside the Erasmus+ funded project HEGenAI. The full thesis title is Design, Implementation, and Impact Evaluation of Generative AI Training for Assessment and Feedback in Higher Education: A Design-Based Research Study. Both the pilot and the training feed back into the design of the training.

Questions? Write to Edda: s250265@dtu.dk

This is a research and training pilot, not a formal degree programme.
