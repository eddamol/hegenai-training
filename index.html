Sign up (1 min)

What does taking part involve?

Sign up, join one or more sessions where you do a module, answer short surveys before and after, and do a short activity to hand in within 2 weeks from the session.

Who can join?

Anyone teaching in higher education, in any discipline and with any level of GenAI experience.

What do I get?

Learn one practical way to use GenAI in assessment or feedback, and keep what you make with it.

What happens to my data?

Taking part is voluntary and you can withdraw at any time. Progress and self-check answers stay in your own browser. Details are in the privacy notice.

Do I need to join the study to use the modules?

No. The study is a separate, optional way to take part.

Who is behind this?

A master's thesis at DTU, part of the Erasmus+ HEGenAI project. More in About & Credits.

This is a research and training pilot, not a formal degree programme.
