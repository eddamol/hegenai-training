Privacy Notice

How your data is handled if you take part in the pilot or the training

The Project

This website is set up by me, Edda Möller, an MSc student at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU). The pilot and the training are part of my master's thesis on training higher education educators to use generative AI in assessment and feedback, supervised by Md Saifuddin Khalid (DTU Compute). The work is connected to HEGenAI, a project that received funding from the EU's Erasmus Plus programme (grant agreement 2025-1-DK01-KA220-HED-000364628). DTU is responsible for the data (the data controller).

This website

This website collects no personal data. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, or tracking. The hosting service may log visitors' IP addresses for security, but in that case, I do not have access to them. The sign-up button opens a SurveyXact survey in a new window. The website does not collect what you enter there.

What Is Collected from You as a Participant

  • Sign-up: Your email address, discipline and institution, and whether you want the pilot, the training or either. This is collected in a separate SurveyXact survey that opens in a new window when you click the sign-up button. I use the answers to select a varied group of participants and to send you information and the consent form. Signing up does not mean you agree to take part in the research. If you do not take part, your sign-up answers are deleted when recruitment closes.
  • Pilot and training: Your consent, surveys, and my notes on how the sessions run. The notes are about the session, not about you. Where your answers need to be linked, for example before and after the training, I use a participant code, not your name. Sessions may be online, in person or hybrid.
  • Certification: If you choose to be certified, the work you submit and a record of your certification. The work should not include identifiable information about you or your students.
  • To stay in touch: Your name, email address, main role, and institution, and a record of your attendance.
  • Not collected: No recordings and no sensitive personal data.

Reason for the Data Collected

To find out how well the training works and how the effect of training can be measured. Results are reported for the group, so no single participant can be recognised in the thesis.

Participation Is a Choice

You can take the training, and get certified, without taking part in the research. You can withdraw at any time without giving a reason. Anonymous data, such as session notes and anonymous surveys, cannot be traced back to you, so it cannot be removed once collected. Other data is deleted on request until analysis starts.

Where Data is Kept and Who Can See It

Data is collected in SurveyXact, DTU's survey tool, and stored in DTU's OneDrive. Only I and my supervisor see your answers, and a research assistant working on the HEGenAI project may see your name and email to help organise sessions. If you choose to be certified, a reviewer sees the work you submit, under your participant code and without your name. Your data is not shared outside DTU in any form that identifies you, and it is not put into AI tools.

How long it is kept

Everything is deleted once my thesis is graded, and before my DTU account closes. The only exception is if you agree, separately, that your coded data may be reused in later research.

Your Rights

You can ask to see the data I hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or withdraw your consent. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Questions

This notice may change as the study develops. You will see the current version before you give consent.
