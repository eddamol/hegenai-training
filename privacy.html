Privacy Notice

How your data is handled if you take part in the pilot or the training

The Project

This website is set up by me, Edda Möller, an MSc student at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU). The pilot and the training are part of my master's thesis on training higher education educators to use generative AI in assessment and feedback, supervised by Md Saifuddin Khalid (DTU Compute). The work is connected to HEGenAI, a project that received funding from the EU's Erasmus Plus programme (grant agreement 2025-1-DK01-KA220-HED-000364628). As the student carrying out the thesis, I am responsible for your data (the data controller).

This website

This website collects no personal data. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, or tracking. The hosting service may log visitors' IP addresses for security, but in that case, I do not have access to them. The sign-up button opens a SurveyXact survey in a new window. The website does not collect what you enter there.

The training pages remember which blocks you have marked as done and which self-check answers you gave, in your own browser only. This is never sent to me or anyone else, and I cannot see it. You can clear it with “Reset my progress” on the Modules page or by clearing your browser's site data.

What Is Collected from You as a Participant

  • Sign-up: Your email address, discipline, institution and event choice (the pilot, the training or either). This is collected in a separate SurveyXact survey that opens in a new window when you click the sign-up button. I use the answers to select a varied group of participants and to contact you. I see them, and possibly my supervisor and a research assistant from the HEGenAI project who helps organise sessions. They are stored in DTU's SurveyXact and DTU's OneDrive. Signing up is not consent to the research. I ask for that separately later, and you can take the training without joining the research. If you do not take part, your sign-up answers are deleted when recruitment closes.
  • Pilot and training: Your consent, surveys, and my notes on how the sessions run. The notes are about the session, not about you. Where your answers need to be linked, for example before and after the training, I use a participant code, not your name. Sessions may be online, in person or hybrid.
  • Certification: If you choose to be certified, the work you submit and a record of your certification. The work should not include identifiable information about you or your students.
  • To stay in touch: Your name, email address, main role, and institution, and a record of your attendance.
  • Not collected: No recordings and no sensitive personal data.

Reason for the Data Collected

To write my thesis: to find out how well the training works and how the effect of training can be measured. Results are reported for the group, so no single participant can be recognised in the thesis.

Legal Basis

I process your data only with your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation). Before you take part, you get written information, and the surveys ask separately for your consent to take part and to the processing of your data.

Participation Is a Choice

You can take the training, and get certified, without taking part in the research. You can withdraw your consent at any time without giving a reason, by emailing me. After that, I stop processing your data and delete it. Results already in the thesis stay only in a form that does not identify you. Anonymous data, such as session notes and anonymous surveys, cannot be traced back to you, so it cannot be removed.

Where Data is Kept and Who Can See It

Data is collected in SurveyXact, DTU's survey tool, and stored in DTU's OneDrive. It is treated confidentially. Only I and my supervisor see your answers, and a research assistant working on the HEGenAI project may see your name and email to help organise sessions. If you choose to be certified, a reviewer sees the work you submit, under your participant code and without your name. DTU and the external examiner may see data needed to supervise and assess the thesis. Your data is not shared with anyone else unless you consent to it, and it is not put into AI tools.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept until my thesis has been assessed and the time for complaints about the assessment has passed. It is then deleted or anonymised as soon as possible. The only exception is if you agree, separately, that your coded data may be reused in later research.

Your Rights

You can ask me for a copy of the data I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or withdraw your consent. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) at dt@datatilsynet.dk.

Questions

Feel free to direct any questions, suggestions, or other thoughts to me, Edda Möller: s250265@dtu.dk

This notice may change as the study develops. You will see the current version before you give consent.

This is a research and training pilot, not a formal degree programme.
