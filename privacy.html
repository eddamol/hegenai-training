Privacy Notice

How your data is handled if you take part in the pilot or the training

The Project

This website is set up by me, Edda Möller, an MSc student at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU). The pilot and the training are part of my master's thesis on training higher education educators to use generative AI in assessment and feedback, supervised by Md Saifuddin Khalid (DTU Compute). The work is connected to HEGenAI, a project that received funding from the EU's Erasmus Plus programme (grant agreement 2025-1-DK01-KA220-HED-000364628). DTU is responsible for the data (the data controller).

This website

This website collects no personal data. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, or tracking. The hosting service may log visitors' IP addresses for security, but in that case, I do not have access to them. The sign-up button opens a SurveyXact survey in a new window. The website does not collect what you enter there.

What Is Collected from You as a Participant

  • Pilot (November 2nd, 2026): Your consent, my notes on how the session runs, and a short survey afterwards. The notes are about the session, not about you, and the survey is anonymous.
  • Training (November 16th and 26th, 2026): Your consent, a survey before and after the training, the evidence you submit for certification, and attendance. Your answers are linked by a participant code, not by your name. The submission for certification should not include identifiable information about you or your students.
  • Sign-up: If you sign up your interest, your email address and whether you want the pilot, the training or both. This is collected in a separate SurveyXact survey that opens in a new window when you click the sign-up button. I use the address only to send you information and the consent form. If you do not take part, it is deleted when recruitment closes on November 16th, 2026.
  • To stay in touch: Your name, email address, main role, and institution.
  • Not collected: No recordings and no sensitive personal data.

Reason for the Data Collected

To find out how well the training works and how the effect of training can be measured. Results are reported for the group, so no single participant can be recognised in the thesis.

Participation Is a Choice

You can take the training, and get certified, without taking part in the research. You can withdraw at any time without giving a reason. Anonymous pilot notes and survey answers cannot be traced back to you, so they cannot be removed once collected. Training data is deleted on request until analysis starts.

Where Data is Kept and Who Can See It

Data is collected in SurveyXact, DTU's survey tool, and stored in DTU's OneDrive. Only I and my supervisor see your answers, and a research assistant working on the HEGenAI project may see your name and email to help organise sessions. Your data is not shared outside DTU in any form that identifies you, and it is not put into AI tools.

How long it is kept

Everything is deleted once my thesis is graded, and no later than March 31st, 2027. The only exception is if you agree, separately, that your coded data may be reused in later research.

Your Rights

You can ask to see the data I hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or withdraw your consent. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Questions
