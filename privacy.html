Privacy Notice

How your data is handled if you take part in the training or help test it

The Project

This website is set up by me, Edda Möller, an MSc student at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU). The training sessions and the testing of the training are part of my master's thesis on training higher education educators to use generative AI in assessment and feedback, supervised by Md Saifuddin Khalid at DTU Compute. The work is connected to HEGenAI, a project that received funding from the EU's Erasmus Plus programme (grant agreement 2025-1-DK01-KA220-HED-000364628). DTU is responsible for data collected for the study, i.e. is the data controller, since it is part of a DTU research project. My supervisor, Md Saifuddin Khalid, is responsible for the data at DTU. I handle the data for the purpose of my thesis.

This website

This website collects no personal data. It has no accounts, cookies, or tracking. The hosting service may log visitors' IP addresses for security, but in that case, I do not have access to them. The sign-up button opens a SurveyXact survey in a new window. The website does not collect anything you enter there.

The training pages remember which blocks you have marked as done and which self-check answers you gave, in your own browser only. This is never sent to me or anyone else, and I cannot see it. You can clear it with “Reset my progress” on the Modules page or by clearing your browser's site data.

Text you type into the forms in a module, and the checklist ticks, are also kept only in your browser. They are never sent anywhere. Use ‘Clear’ to remove them.

What Is Collected from You as a Participant

  • Sign-up: Your email address, discipline, institution, and the session you prefer. This is collected in a separate SurveyXact survey. I use the answers to send invitations to the sessions and to send surveys before and after the training (three per session). I see them, and so does my supervisor, and possibly a research assistant from the HEGenAI project who helps organise sessions. Surveys are stored in DTU's SurveyXact and responses are exported to DTU's OneDrive. I keep your email address in a list with a participant code (for example P001), separate from your answers. Signing up is not consent to the research. I ask for that separately later, and you can take the training without joining the research. Your sign-up answers are deleted together with your other contact details (see “How long it is kept” below).
  • Training sessions: Your consent, your survey answers, the practice log or other work you choose to hand in as evidence of your practice, and my notes on how the sessions run. The notes are about the session, not about individuals. Where your answers need to be linked, for example before and after the training, I use your participant code, not names. The code list is kept separate from answers.
  • Feedback conversations: If you take part in a short conversation about the training and the surveys, I take written notes (no recording). The notes carry your participant code or no identifier at all, never names, and they are covered by research consent.
  • Certification (if offered): If a certificate of completion is offered, I check the practice log or other work you hand in against set criteria and keep a record that it is completed. For the certificate itself, I ask you in advance for the name to put on it. Your work should not include identifiable information about you or your students.
  • To stay in touch: Your email address, your name where it appears in an invitation or where you give it to me (for example for a certificate), and a record of your attendance. Invitations and reminders are sent so that participants cannot see each other's names or addresses.
  • Recordings: Part of a session may be recorded by the training team in DTU's Panopto, to capture the material presented and not the participants. I do not make recordings for my research or use any recording as research data. The recording itself is handled under DTU's own rules. Email my supervisor (skhalid@dtu.dk) if you have questions or concerns about it.
  • Not collected: No sensitive personal data.

Reason for the Data Collected

Data is collected for me to write my thesis and for the research of the HEGenAI project - to study how well the training works and how the effect of training can be measured. Results are reported for the group, so no single participant can be recognised in the thesis or in the project's reports.

Legal Basis

I process your data only with your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation). Before you take part, you get written information and give one consent, to take part and to the processing of your data as described here.

Participation Is a Choice

You can take the training, and get a certificate if one is offered, without taking part in the research. You can withdraw your consent at any time without giving a reason, by emailing my supervisor, Md Saifuddin Khalid (skhalid@dtu.dk). After that, your data is deleted and no longer processed. Results already in the thesis stay only in a form that does not identify you. Anonymous data, such as session notes, cannot be traced back to you, so it cannot be removed. The same applies to coded answers once the code list has been deleted (see below).

Where Data is Kept and Who Can See It

Data is collected in SurveyXact, DTU's survey tool, and stored in DTU's OneDrive. It is treated confidentially. I and my supervisor, who is responsible for the data, can see your answers and contact details, and a research assistant working on the HEGenAI project may see your name and email to help organise sessions. If certification is offered, whoever checks the work you hand in (I, or a reviewer if there is one) sees it under your participant code and without your name. DTU and the external examiner may see data needed to supervise and assess the thesis. Your data is not shared with anyone else, and it is not put into AI tools.

How long it is kept

Your answers and notes, identified only by a code, are kept at DTU until at least the end of the HEGenAI project (31 August 2028), before they are deleted or anonymised. Your contact details, the code list, consent records, and any work you handed in are deleted earlier, as soon as possible after my thesis has been assessed and the time for complaints about the assessment has passed (planned by end of February 2027). After that, the remaining coded answers can no longer be traced to you.

Your Rights

You can ask my supervisor, Md Saifuddin Khalid (skhalid@dtu.dk), for a copy of the data held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent. You can also contact DTU's data protection officer at dpo@dtu.dk and complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) at dt@datatilsynet.dk.

Contact

Feel free to direct any questions about the study, suggestions, or other thoughts to me, Edda Möller: s250265@dtu.dk. For anything about your data (a copy, correction, deletion or withdrawing your consent), contact my supervisor, Md Saifuddin Khalid: skhalid@dtu.dk.

This notice may change as the study develops. You will see the current version before you give consent.

This is a research and training pilot, not a formal degree programme.
