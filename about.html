About this site

HEGenAI Training offers short, practical modules for educators in higher education on using generative AI in assessment and feedback. Each module takes about an hour and focuses on one concrete thing you can use in your own teaching.

The site is open to educators from any institution or discipline. It is also a research site: the modules are being tested and improved as part of a master's thesis (see below). You can take a module without taking part in the research.

There are no accounts. The site remembers your progress in your own browser only; see the privacy notice.

The project behind it

HEGenAI Training is run by Edda Möller, an MSc student at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU), supervised by Md Saifuddin Khalid (DTU Compute). It is part of her master's thesis, Design, Implementation, and Impact Evaluation of Generative AI Training for Assessment and Feedback in Higher Education: A Design-Based Research Study. The pilot and the training feed back into the design of the modules.

The thesis is carried out alongside HEGenAI (Generative AI for Assessment and Feedback in Higher Education), an Erasmus+ project [link to the project website to be added]. HEGenAI is the context for the work; the design and study of the training are the thesis.

Questions? Write to Edda: s250265@dtu.dk

Funding

HEGenAI Training is part of the Erasmus+ project Generative AI for Assessment and Feedback in Higher Education (project code 2025-1-DK01-KA220-HED-000364628), which is funded by the European Union. The grant is managed by Uddannelses- og Forskningsstyrelsen, the Danish National Agency. The acknowledgement and disclaimer appear in the footer of every page.
