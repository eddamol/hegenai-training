About & Credits

HEGenAI Training offers short, hands-on modules for anyone who teaches in higher education and wants to use generative AI in assessment and feedback. Each module takes about an hour and leaves trainees with something to use in their own practice, such as a declaration guide, a feedback agent or a rubric. No account or prior AI experience is needed.

Part of HEGenAI

The platform is a deliverable of the Erasmus+ project HEGenAI – Generative AI in Higher Education Assessment & Feedback, co-funded by the European Union (2025-1-DK01-KA220-HED-000364628). The project partners are:

  • Technical University of Denmark (DTU), Denmark
  • Chalmers University of Technology, Sweden
  • Brandenburg University of Technology Cottbus-Senftenberg, Germany
  • Mediterranean University of Albania, Albania
  • University of Elbasan "Aleksandër Xhuvani", Albania
  • Palestine Technical University – Kadoorie, Palestine
  • An-Najah National University, Palestine

The making of HEGenAI Training

This platform was designed and built at DTU as part of the MSc thesis Design, Implementation, and Impact Evaluation of Generative AI Training for Assessment and Feedback in Higher Education: A Design-Based Research Study by Edda Möller, supervised by Md Saifuddin Khalid (DTU Compute). Md Jahidul Islam, also an MSc student at DTU, set up two of the first four modules: Making a GenAI Teaching Assistant for Formative Feedback and Creating Rubrics and Feedback Agents with GenAI.

The first four modules were tested with educators in four live sessions in November 2026, online and at DTU Lyngby. Participants took a module and could earn a certificate of completion by handing in a short practice log afterwards. The sessions were evaluated with an impact evaluation instrument, also developed in the HEGenAI project. Feedback from the participants helped shape the modules and the platform as they are today.
